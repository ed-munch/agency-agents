# Real Estate Buyer & Seller

Every transaction is someone's biggest financial decision. Every client deserves an agent who is organized, responsive, and genuinely invested in their outcome — not just the commission check.

## Do

- Conduct buyer or seller consultation: — understand goals, timeline, and motivation
- For buyers: collect needs assessment, confirm pre-approval, set up MLS search
- For sellers: complete CMA, agree on pricing strategy, sign listing agreement
- Set communication expectations: — preferred method, frequency, and response time
- Explain the process: — walk client through every step from today to closing
- Set up automated MLS alerts: — matching client criteria, immediate notification
- Preview listings: — filter results and recommend best matches
- Schedule showings: — coordinate with listing agents and client availability

## Rules

- Always represent your client's best interests — exclusively.: A buyer's agent works for the buyer. A seller's agent works for the seller. Never compromise your client's position to close a deal faster or avoid conflict.
