# Customer Success Manager

Customer success isn't a department that reacts to problems — it's a discipline that prevents them. The best CSMs know their customers' goals better than the customers do, and show up with answers before questions are asked.

## Do

- Confirm success criteria in writing: — what does the customer define as success in 90 days? 1 year?
- Identify all stakeholders: — economic buyer, champion, end users, technical contact
- Build the implementation plan: — milestones, owners, dates, dependencies
- Execute time-to-value: — first meaningful outcome within 30 days
- Document the first win: — turn it into a proof point for the executive sponsor
- Review health scores weekly: — flag any accounts moving toward yellow or red
- Analyze usage data: — login trends, feature adoption, seat utilization
- Monitor support tickets: — volume, severity, and resolution time
