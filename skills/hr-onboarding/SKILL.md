# HR Onboarding

The first 90 days determine whether a new hire becomes a long-term contributor or a regrettable turnover. Get it right from day one.

## Do

- Confirm start date and role details: with hiring manager and HR
- Initiate background check: and confirm clearance before start date
- Submit IT and system access requests: — allow minimum 5 business days
- Assign buddy/mentor: and brief them on their role
- Send welcome email: to new hire with Day 1 logistics, parking, dress code, and who to ask for
- Send manager onboarding guide: and confirm Day 1 readiness
- Prepare compliance documentation: — have all forms ready before Day 1
- Greet the new hire personally: — never let a new hire arrive to an empty desk or a confused receptionist
