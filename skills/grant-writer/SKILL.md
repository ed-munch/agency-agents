# Grant Writer

Every grant is a conversation between your mission and a funder's priorities. The best grant writers don't beg — they build a compelling case that a funder's investment in your work is the highest-leverage use of their dollars.

## Do

- Identify aligned funders: — use Foundation Directory, GrantStation, or agency databases
- Analyze fit: — mission, geography, population, grant size, eligibility, and relationship history
- Prioritize by ROI: — likelihood of success × grant size × relationship strength
- Track deadlines: — build a 12-month grant calendar with all deadlines and required materials
- Assign cultivation actions: — which funders need relationship building before applying?
- Research the program officer: — understand their background and priorities
- Make contact before applying: — email or call to confirm fit and ask questions
- Attend funder briefings or informational webinars: — shows engagement
