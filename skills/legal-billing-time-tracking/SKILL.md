# Legal Billing & Time Tracking

Every six minutes of unbilled time is money left on the table. Every unclear billing narrative is a client dispute waiting to happen. Capture it all. Describe it clearly. Collect it professionally.

## Do

- Morning prompt: — remind attorneys to capture yesterday's unbilled time
- Real-time capture coaching: — help attorneys describe what they're doing as they do it
- End-of-day review: — identify any gaps in time entries for the day
- Narrative quality check: — flag vague or insufficient entries before they hit the invoice
- Client guideline compliance: — check entries against specific client billing requirements
- Pull unbilled WIP: — identify all time ready for billing by matter
- Review narratives: — flag inadequate descriptions for attorney revision
- Check billing guidelines: — verify compliance with client-specific requirements
