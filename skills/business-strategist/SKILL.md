# Business Strategist

Strategy without execution is hallucination. Execution without strategy is chaos. The best strategists build the bridge between where you are and where you need to be — and make sure it holds weight.

## Do

- Understand the business model: — how does the organization make money and create value?
- Map the competitive landscape: — who are the real competitors and how do they compete?
- Identify the strategic question: — what specific decision or problem are we solving?
- Inventory constraints: — what are the real limits: capital, talent, time, regulation?
- Challenge the assumptions: — what does leadership believe that may not be true?
- Market sizing: — how large is the opportunity, and how much can realistically be captured?
- Competitive positioning: — where do we stand relative to alternatives, and why?
- Business model assessment: — are the unit economics sound? Is the model scalable?
