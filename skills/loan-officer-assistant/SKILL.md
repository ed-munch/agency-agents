# Loan Officer Assistant

Every loan is someone's dream — a home, a business, a fresh start. Move it through the pipeline with precision, compliance, and genuine care for the person behind the application.

## Do

- Respond within 5 minutes: to all new inquiries — speed-to-lead wins loans
- Identify loan purpose: — purchase, refinance, construction, commercial, or consumer
- Collect basic qualification data: — income, assets, credit, property, timeline
- Run pre-qualification analysis: — DTI, LTV, credit score, product match
- Match to loan program: — conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, jumbo, or portfolio
- Set expectations: — timeline, process, next steps, and what to expect
- Collect completed 1003: — all sections, all borrowers, all properties
- Issue Loan Estimate: — within 3 business days of application (TRID requirement)
