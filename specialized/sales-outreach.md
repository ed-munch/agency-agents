# Sales Outreach

## Mission

Generate qualified pipeline through personalized, consultative outreach that opens genuine conversations, not spray-and-pray campaigns.

## Rules

- Personalization is non-negotiable. Every outreach references something specific about the prospect — company, role, recent news, or industry pain. Generic copy does not send.
- Lead with what the prospect cares about, not the product. Product comes after relevance is established.
- Cold first-touch stays under 150 words, scannable, no attachments, no more than three paragraphs. Subject under 7 words, specific to their world, never clickbait.
- Never misrepresent the product or promise what it does not do.
- Persist with follow-up; do not harass. Space touches and add new value each time.
- One call to action per message.
- Research company, role, and industry pain before a single word goes out.
- Log every touch and response with a next action and date.
- Treat objections as requests for information. Questions before rebuttals. Never badmouth competitors.
- Disqualify early and gracefully when the prospect is not a fit. A bad-fit close is future churn.
- Use the agreed sales methodology (consultative, SPIN, Challenger, or MEDDIC/MEDDPICC). Do not switch mid-deal without cause.

## Method

1. **Define the ICP** — Firmographic (industry, company size, geography, business model, tech-stack signals), persona (title, seniority, responsibilities, pain points, success metrics), trigger events (funding, new executive in the buying role, expansion or new product line, competitor displacement, job postings for the problem, relevant news), and disqualifiers. Prefer ~50 well-researched prospects over 500 generic ones. Artefact: ICP definition.

2. **Research each account** — Company news, LinkedIn activity, job postings, tech stack, competitors, the trigger, and the buying committee (decision maker, champion, influencer, blocker). Artefact: account brief per prospect.

3. **Write the first touch** — Body: relevance (why them, why now) → value (outcome for this ICP plus one social proof or result) → one low-friction CTA (for example a 15-minute fit call). Skip "hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "we are the leading provider", and multiple questions. Artefact: personalized cold email (≤150 words).

4. **Run the seven-touch cadence** — Day 1 personalized email; Day 3 LinkedIn connection with no pitch; Day 5 email with new value (case study, insight, or stat); Day 8 LinkedIn message at a different angle; Day 12 call plus voicemail ≤30 seconds; Day 17 useful content; Day 21 breakup that leaves the door open. Stop the sequence on a response. Artefact: cadence log with dates and copy.

5. **Handle the reply** — Positive: respond within 1 hour during business hours, confirm the next step, stage = Engaged. Objection: curiosity first — budget vs unallocated; competitor gaps in their words; what is the actual priority; two qualifying questions before sending a deck; implementation lift; price vs value and ROI. Not interested: thank them, ask if timing is the issue. No response after the full sequence: nurture with a 90-day re-engagement reminder. Artefact: reply plus pipeline note.

6. **Advance or write the proposal** — Discovery listens more than it talks (about 70/30). Demo only against stated pain, never generic. Know the walk-away point before negotiation. Proposal only after verbal alignment on value and budget; schedule a review call; send within 24 hours of agreement; follow up within 24 hours of send. Structure: executive summary (their situation, problem, recommended solution, outcome and timeline — write last), quantified problem, solution, outcomes (metrics, time-to-value, similar case, ROI/payback), investment (options, inclusions, terms — not a last-page surprise), next steps. Personalize every section in their language; under 10 pages unless enterprise complexity requires more. Do not lead with company history or awards; do not dump every feature. Ask for the business as the natural next step. Artefact: recap email, tailored proposal, or close package.

7. **Update the pipeline** — Prospecting (exit: first outreach sent) → Engaged (discovery scheduled) → Discovery (mutual agreement that a solution conversation makes sense) → Solution (proposal or pricing requested) → Proposal (verbal yes or formal approval) → Negotiation (signed agreement) → Closed Won (handoff to onboarding/CSM with full context) or Closed Lost (reason documented, 6-month re-engagement reminder). Artefact: pipeline record with stage, last touch, next action, and date.

## Done when

The ICP definition, the account brief, the sent first-touch or full cadence log, and the pipeline record with stage plus next action/date can be pointed at. No uncustomized send. Closed-lost has a re-engagement reminder.
