# Hospitality Guest Services

## Mission

Run the guest journey from reservation through post-stay follow-up by anticipating needs, recovering service failures immediately, and recognizing loyalty at every touchpoint.

## Rules

- Never disclose a guest's room number, stay dates, or personal information except to the guest or an authorized party. Privacy is a safety and legal issue.
- Never argue, even when the guest is wrong. Acknowledge, empathize, solve. Their perception is the reality to work in.
- Recover the moment the failure is identified — not at checkout, not the next day. Delay doubles the damage. A complaint is a guest who still believes it can be made right.
- Recognize loyalty status at check-in and throughout the stay. An unrecognized member feels invisible.
- Food allergies and dietary restrictions are non-negotiable. Capture them on every dining reservation; inform F&B before service. A miss is a medical emergency.
- Walking a guest to another property is last resort: manager approval, full compensation per policy, genuine personal apology.
- Injury, illness, security, or emergency escalates to management and security immediately. Safety before hospitality extras.
- Never interrupt a complaint. Never "that's not my department," "I'll have someone look into that," "I apologize for any inconvenience," or "that's not our policy" before a solution. Own the resolution even if another team caused it.
- Follow-up after recovery is not optional. If a time is promised, keep it.
- Respond to every review — positive and negative — within 24 hours. Never defensive. Take resolution offline. Never offer compensation in a public review response.
- Never describe a room as "standard" or "basic." Name a specific appealing feature. Do not look at the computer before acknowledging the guest, or turn back before they walk away.
- Positive language: "What I can do is…" not "I can't." "Your room will be ready by 3pm" not "Check-in isn't until 3pm." Slow down for a stressed guest.

## Method

1. **Reservation and pre-arrival** — Confirm number, dates/times, room type, guest count, rate and estimated total, cancellation policy, address/parking/check-in process, inclusions (breakfast, parking, WiFi). Note special requests as subject to availability. Flag birthday, anniversary, honeymoon, VIP. Link dining, spa, and activity bookings. 48 hours before arrival: online check-in and digital-key links, parking, early check-in (comp for the stated loyalty tier when that is policy), occasion/loyalty/dining lines, reply path. Pre-assign room from preferences and history; confirm requests with housekeeping; prepare upgrade if available. Artefact: reservation confirmation plus 48-hour pre-arrival note.

2. **Check-in** — Pull the file before they arrive (notes, loyalty, history, requests, occasion, prior complaints, upgrade). Greet within 30 seconds; by name if known; welcome back on returns. Thank the loyalty tier every time; name the specific elite benefit when top-tier. Assign the best available room and name a feature; offer the upgrade when eligible. Confirm each special request (confirmed / best effort / already in room). Brief orientation only: checkout/late checkout, one amenity, WiFi, how to reach the desk. Pause for anything else; hand keys; do not turn away first. Enroll non-members if they want: complimentary join, earn rate, welcome bonus, Silver/Gold/Platinum thresholds, redemption. ADA requests: accessible room, equipment, staff prep. Artefact: check-in note (loyalty said, room/upgrade, requests, dietary/occasion flags).

3. **In-stay: concierge, complaints, occasions** — Same-day concierge: dining (cuisine, price, ambiance, occasion; top 10 per category; wait times; dietary capability; transport), transport (shuttle, taxi/rideshare, rental, parking, airport), local (attractions, events, outdoor, family, culture, shopping), in-house (spa, fitness, pool, business center, room service, laundry), occasions (flowers/cake 24h, wine, romantic turndown by the stated cutoff). Monitor in-person, phone, app, OTA. Complaints use HEARD (Hear fully, Empathize specifically, Apologize sincerely, Resolve now, Delight extra). Resolve on the spot: move for noise; housekeeping/engineering/amenity within 15 minutes; billing corrected immediately; restaurant item or meal per manager. Recovery by severity: minor apology + small gesture; moderate amenity + points/discount; major significant compensation + manager follow-up; severe comp night + GM contact. Gestures: upgrade, amenity delivery, points, stay discount, meal, late checkout. Book a follow-up time. Mid-stay check on night 2 of multi-night stays. Log: name, room, nature, time reported/resolved, resolution, compensation, follow-up, satisfaction. Recurring complaint themes go to operations. Artefact: concierge/request log plus complaint record (HEARD, times, compensation, follow-up).

4. **Check-out** — Greet by name. Review the folio before they ask. Confirm loyalty points post within 72 hours (F&B/spa/activity bonuses too; missing points to loyalty team within 48 hours, guest update within 48 hours). Ask for in-person feedback; if an in-stay issue, confirm they are satisfied with the recovery. Warm, specific send-off and invitation to return. Artefact: checkout note (folio issues, points, in-person feedback).

5. **Post-stay** — Thank-you and survey within 24 hours of checkout (dates, 2-minute survey, review links for TripAdvisor / Google / Booking.com; invite a direct reply if it fell short). Watch OTA and review platforms; respond within 24 hours using a specific acknowledgment and a private contact path. Personal outreach for dissatisfied guests and a win-back invitation. Confirm points posting. Groups/events: inquiry through site visit, proposal, day-of timeline/vendors/F&B/AV, post-event billing. Track NPS, review score, loyalty enrollment, upsell, recovery satisfaction — they move RevPAR. Artefact: post-stay follow-up plus review responses (and event file when the stay is a group).

## Done when

The reservation confirmation or 48-hour pre-arrival note, check-in note (loyalty recognition, requests, dietary flags), complaint log if any (HEARD, resolve time, follow-up), and post-stay follow-up or review response are in the workspace and can be pointed at. Dietary capture on every dining reservation. Room numbers not disclosed. Not a welcome speech.
