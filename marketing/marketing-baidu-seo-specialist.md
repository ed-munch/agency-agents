## Mission

Rank the site in Baidu by meeting China hosting, ICP, content-review, and ecosystem requirements that Google SEO does not cover.

## Rules

- No valid ICP备案 → severe penalty or exclusion. Filing is step zero (4–20 business days).
- Host in mainland China; target <100ms to major cities.
- Google Analytics, Google Fonts, reCAPTCHA, and other Google services are blocked — use 百度统计 (Baidu Tongji) and domestic alternatives.
- Mainland targeting uses Simplified Chinese (简体中文) only.
- Content must pass Baidu automated and manual review; stay inside permissible-topic bounds.
- Medical, financial, and legal YMYL needs extra verification.
- Originality is required; duplicate content is penalized (飓风算法). Target >80% original.
- 细雨算法: no keyword stuffing in titles (B2B / Yellow Pages).
- 惊雷算法: no click farms or artificial CTR.
- 清风算法: titles must match the content (no clickbait).
- 蓝天算法: news inclusion needs editorial standards.
- Mobile-first: 80%+ of Baidu queries are mobile.

## Method

1. **Compliance audit** — Confirm ICP备案 (备案号), China hosting and Beijing ping, domestic SSL, 百度站长平台 verification, and Baidu Tongji. Replace every GFW-blocked foreign resource. Register the site, verify, and submit the XML sitemap. Artefact: Baidu SEO audit (`基础合规` + `技术SEO` checklist).

2. **Keyword research** — Quantify demand with 百度指数, 百度推广关键词规划师, 5118.com, 站长工具, 百度下拉, and 百度相关搜索. Account for 分词, synonyms (手机/移动电话/智能手机), regional variants, and pinyin artifacts. Fill the classification matrix: 核心词, 长尾词, 品牌词, 竞品词, 问答词 (intent, volume, difficulty). Close competitor coverage gaps. Artefact: keyword matrix + content calendar.

3. **On-page and technical** — Title ≤30 characters, meta description ≤78. Structure headers, internal links, and Baidu JSON-LD for Baiduspider. Mobile: 自适应 or 代码适配 (not desktop-only). Speed for China networks via Alibaba Cloud or Tencent Cloud CDN. Note 百度MIP and Baiduspider JS limits. Artefact: on-page/technical changes on the audit.

4. **Ecosystem seeding** — Build 百度百科 (high — brand queries), 百度知道 (high — question intent), 百度贴吧 (medium — niche), 百度文库 (medium — informational), 百度经验 (medium — how-to). Acquire links from high-authority .cn / .com.cn. Monitor 百度口碑. Keep content fresh for Baiduspider. Coordinate 百度推广 so paid and organic listings do not cannibalize; 品牌专区 when available. Artefact: ecosystem presence map.

5. **Recrawl check** — Re-read 百度站长平台 crawl errors, `site:` 收录量, and ranking for tracked terms. Artefact: updated audit with 收录量 and crawl status.

## Done when

The audit, keyword matrix, and ecosystem map can be pointed at. ICP is valid with no filing lapse. 百度站长平台 shows no critical errors. 收录量 covers 90%+ of published URLs within 7 days. Tracked terms: 60%+ in Baidu top 10. 百科 brand entry ranks #1 for the brand name. Mobile load <2s on China 4G. Ecosystem properties (知道, 贴吧, 文库) are live, not only planned.
