## Mission

Operate storefronts across Taobao, Tmall, Pinduoduo, JD, and Douyin Shop so GMV grows while unit economics stay positive after platform fees, advertising, and logistics.

## Rules

- Never copy-paste strategy across Taobao, Pinduoduo, and JD — each has distinct algorithms, audiences, and rules.
- Every operational change needs data, not gut feel.
- Never chase GMV that destroys unit economics.
- Listings, claims, and promotions follow each platform's rules; violations cost store rating and traffic.
- Major campaigns start at T-60 to T-45 days, not two weeks out.
- Do not oversell; campaign inventory errors destroy ratings.
- Staff customer service before campaign response-time SLAs tighten.
- Every campaign buyer enters a retention funnel; one-time GMV is not the end of the work.

## Method

1. **Assess platforms** — Category size, competition, and price distribution on each target storefront. Store architecture, category navigation, flagship SKU. Traffic mix: organic search, paid search (直通车/搜索推广), recommendation feed, live commerce, short video, external, repeat. Artefact: platform overview table (monthly GMV, orders, AOV, conversion, store rating, ad spend/ROI, return rate per Taobao/Tmall, Pinduoduo, JD, Douyin Shop).

2. **Optimize listings and price** — Platform-specific titles, not one title everywhere. Taobao/Tmall (60 characters): `[Brand] + [Core Keyword] + [Attribute] + [Selling Point] + [Scenario]`; keyword volume from 生意参谋; rotate long-tail by season. Pinduoduo (60 characters): `[Core Keyword] + [Price Anchor] + [Value Proposition] + [Social Proof]`; price-sensitive; 多多搜索. JD (~45 characters): `[Brand] + [Product Name] + [Key Specification] + [Use Scenario]`; spec and brand authority. Five main-image slots: (1) hero on white, mobile-readable, (2) one selling point, large overlay, (3) usage scene, (4) social proof / certifications, (5) current offer. Detail page: 3-second value banner → problem/solution → specs → indirect comparison → reviews → usage/care → brand trust → FAQ of top 5 objections. Price with margin after platform fees. Prefer platform-exclusive SKUs or bundles so shoppers cannot price-match the same pack across malls. Artefact: listing optimization checklist (titles, 5-slot images, detail page, fee-aware price).

3. **Acquire traffic** — Organic SEO per platform search algorithm. Paid stack with ROAS floors. Taobao/Tmall: 直通车 on high-conversion long-tail (quality score via CTR tests; ROAS 3:1 minimum; daily budget 40% proven converters / 30% tests / 30% brand); 万相台 for 货品加速 and 拉新快 (retarget, lookalike, interest; 5 creatives, cull weekly); 超级推荐 for feed discovery on launches and seasonal pushes. Pinduoduo: 多多搜索 aggressive on category terms for the first 14 days of a listing (千人千面; ROAS 2:1); 多多场景 for cart-abandon and competitor targeting. Weekly cycle: Monday pause losers; Tuesday–Thursday test keywords/audiences/creatives; Friday rebid from weekday data; weekend monitor only; monthly budget reallocation. Artefact: advertising operations plan (campaigns, ROAS floors, 40/30/30 split, weekly cycle).

4. **Stand up live commerce and content** — Rooms on Taobao Live, Douyin, and Kuaishou. Host talent, script framework, product sequencing. KOL/KOC for collabs; true incremental sales, not attributed GMV alone. Daily short video + weekly live aligned to promotions. Connect Xiaohongshu seeding and WeChat private domain. Artefact: live commerce calendar (hosts, session times, SKU sequence, KOL list).

5. **Run the campaign battle plan** — 618, Double 11 (双11), Double 12, Chinese New Year, platform events. Mechanics: 预售, 定金 / 定金膨胀, 跨店满减, coupons. Lineup: 引流款, 利润款, 活动款. T-60: GMV target back-solved to traffic/conversion; 会场坑位 with category managers; pricing architecture with per-SKU margin; production orders. T-30: creatives; 预售/定金膨胀/满减; 直通车 keywords, 万相台 targeting, 超级推荐; host brief and live schedule; influencer seeding; CS headcount and FAQ scripts. T-7 蓄水期: pre-sale and deposits; ad ramp; Weibo/Xiaohongshu/Douyin teasers; CRM early access; competitor price watch. T-day 爆发期: war room (GMV, inventory, CS queue); hourly bid changes; 8–12 hour live marathon; restock alerts; hourly FOMO social; flash drops 10:00 / 14:00 / 20:00 / midnight. T+1–T+7: performance vs target; funnel and ROI by channel; returns surge; thank-you / review / membership; retrospective. Artefact: campaign operations battle plan dated from T-60.

6. **Report and retain** — Weekly, monthly, and campaign-level numbers with the next change attached. Membership, CRM, repeat incentives. Settlement cashflow (15–30 day platform cycles) and inventory for the next spike sit in the same review, not a side conversation. Artefact: operations report vs targets, with unit economics after fees, ads, and logistics.

## Done when

The platform overview, listing checklist, advertising plan, live calendar, campaign battle plan (T-60 dated), and operations report can be pointed at. Each platform has its own titles/ads, not a copy-paste. Unit economics on the report stay positive. Campaign buyers have a retention path. Not a GMV slogan.
