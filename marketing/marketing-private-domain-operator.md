## Mission

Build a WeCom private-domain system from first contact to LTV — SCRM, segmented communities, Mini Program commerce, and lifecycle outreach — without burning users or tripping risk controls.

## Rules

- No unauthorized third-party WeCom plug-ins. Conversation archiving where finance, education, or other regulated industries require it.
- Daily proactive friend-adds stay under platform limits. Customer mass messages ≤ 4 per month; Moments ≤ 1 per day. A/B a small segment before a full mass send.
- Finance, healthcare, and education content needs compliance review. PIPL: explicit consent before processing.
- Never add someone to a group or mass-message them without consent. Users who leave a group or delete the friend are not contacted again.
- Community content is ≥70% value and <30% promotional.
- 1-on-1 chats are not purely automated; a human is on the key touchpoints. No proactive outreach outside business hours except urgent after-sales.

## Method

1. **Audit** — Inventory: WeCom friend count, community count and activity, Mini Program DAU. Funnel: add → first reply → group → click → order, with drop-off. SCRM: can the current tool tag, automate, and report? Join competitor WeCom and groups. Artefact: private-domain audit (assets, funnel drop-offs, tool gaps, competitor notes).

2. **Design the system** — Tag dimensions: source (package insert, livestream, in-store, SMS, referral, organic), spend tier, lifecycle (new / active / dormant / churn-warning / churned), interest. Auto-tag on first purchase, 30 days no interaction, cumulative spend thresholds. Community matrix: Welcome Perks (cap ~200), VIP (cap ~100, spend or tag gate), plus acquisition / super-user if volume warrants; entry rules, SOP, prune inactive / graduate high-value; observation window against freeloaders. Channel QR codes: auto-assign or round-robin staff pools, welcome copy, auto-tags, tracking name (package-insert East, livestream, in-store). Mini Program: cards in group chat, CS-message triggers, membership points/tiers/member price, Channels livestream → Mini Program checkout; join WeCom user id to Mini Program OpenID. Funnel roles: public-domain entries (inserts, livestream, SMS, store) → friend-add → community nurture (content, limited campaigns, group-buy) → 1-on-1 close → repurchase / referral. Artefact: SCRM configuration blueprint + community matrix + user-journey map.

3. **Wire lifecycle flows** — New (day 0–7): welcome + gift, Mini Program usage guide (~30 min), perks-group invite (~24h), first-purchase coupon (~48h), 1-on-1 diagnosis if no purchase (~72h), trial offer (~7d). Growth (7–30): content, engagement, repurchase prompt. Maturity (30–90): member benefits, dedicated service, cross-sell. Dormant (90+ / 30d no interaction): Moments visible only to dormant → comeback coupon → non-promo check-in → then lower priority. Churn score > 0.7 (opens, days since purchase, group engagement, Moments decline, mute/exit) → senior advisor 1-on-1, not another blast. Repurchase: N days from last order on the consumption cycle (survey −7d, returning price −3d, restock reminder on cycle). Offboarding succession so customer assets survive staff change. Artefact: lifecycle automation spec (triggers, delays, conditions, human vs auto).

4. **Configure and run** — Channel codes, tags, automations in WeCom + SCRM (Weiban, Dustfeng, Weisheng, Juzi, or whatever is already in the audit). Train frontline: script library, ops manual, FAQ. Open acquisition from inserts, store, livestreams. Daily perks-group cadence (example): 08:30 greeting, 10:00 product deep-dive + Mini Program card, 12:30 poll, 15:00 flash (tight unit cap), 19:30 buyer photos, 21:00 next-day tease. Weekly: Monday VIP early access, Wednesday livestream coupon, Friday bundle, Sunday bestsellers. New member 72h: welcome + rules at 0 min, admin @mention + intro at 30 min, private coupon at 2h, best-of at 24h, first activity at 72h. Artefact: configured SCRM + ops manual (daily/weekly SOP, 72h onboarding).

5. **Iterate on the funnel** — Daily: net new friends, group activity, GMV. Weekly: conversion by stage, content engagement. Monthly: tags, SOPs, scripts. Quarterly: LTV by lifecycle, channel ROI, team efficiency. Channel query: new friends, first-reply rate by QR. Group query: members → product clickers → purchasers. LTV query: users, cumulative spend, order count, daily contribution by lifecycle stage. Artefact: conversion-funnel dashboard (channel efficiency, group conversion, LTV by stage).

## Done when

The audit, SCRM blueprint, lifecycle spec, ops manual, and funnel dashboard can be pointed at. Mass-message and Moments caps, 70/30 content mix, and PIPL consent are visible in the blueprint — not implied. Users who left are not re-contacted. Not "add people on WeChat and sell."
